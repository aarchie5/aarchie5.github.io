Website Evaluations

Nike

  • Contrast: Nike uses bold headings, large product images, and contrasting colors to draw attention to important information. The difference in text sizes also helps visitors identify featured products and promotions.
  • Repetition: The website maintains consistent fonts, navigation menus, and product layouts throughout its pages. This creates a recognizable design and makes the website easier to navigate.
  • Alignment: Nike organizes its product images, descriptions, and shopping categories into structured layouts. The consistent alignment makes the website look professional and helps users find information quickly.
  • Proximity: Related products, descriptions, and shopping options are grouped together. The spacing between different sections helps visitors distinguish between product categories and promotions.

Kids Dinosaurs

  • Contrast: Kids Dinosaurs lacks a clear visual hierarchy because many links and text elements compete for attention. The limited distinction between important information and secondary content makes the page difficult to scan.
  • Repetition: Although the website repeats navigation links and similar text styles, the repetition does not create an organized appearance. Instead, the repeated menus and crowded content make the website feel cluttered.
  • Alignment: The website's dense arrangement of links, images, and text creates a less structured appearance. The layout could benefit from more consistent alignment and clearly defined content sections.
  • Proximity: The website places numerous links and informational elements close together without enough visual separation. Adding spacing between unrelated sections would make the content easier to understand and navigate.
